Bhagavad Gita 2.47 • Purpose & Dharma

"Karmany evadhikaras te ma phalesu kadacana,
ma karma-phala-hetur bhur ma te sango 'stv akarmani."

diamond

You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of action. Never consider yourself the cause of the results of your activities, and never be attached to not doing your duty.

wb_incandescent Modern Application

In a results-driven professional environment, anxiety arises from obsessing over outcomes (promotions, metrics). Focus entirely on the quality and integrity of your daily effort. Detachment from the result paradoxically leads to better performance and peace of mind.

Bhagavad Gita 6.5 Stress & Anxiety

"Let a man elevate himself by his own mind, not degrade himself. The mind is the friend of the conditioned soul, and his enemy as well."

Application

Recognize negative self-talk as the mind acting as an enemy. Mindfulness and self-regulation turn the mind into an ally against anxiety.

Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda Leadership

"A ruler must be like a father to his subjects, punishing the wicked, protecting the good, and ensuring the welfare of all without discrimination."

Application

True leadership is rooted in service and empathy, not authority. Ensure your team feels protected and supported to foster loyalty.

Katha Upanishad Grief

"The knowing Self is not born, it does not die. It has not sprung from anything, nothing has sprung from it. The Ancient is unborn, eternal, everlasting."

Application

In times of profound loss, finding solace in the imperishable nature of the soul can provide a grounding perspective beyond physical temporary states.
